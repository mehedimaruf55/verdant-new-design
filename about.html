About us

About Verdant ESG

Helping businesses make environmental claims they can support.

Businesses are under growing pressure to explain their environmental impact. Customers, investors and commercial partners want clear information. Turning complex sustainability work into accurate public statements takes care.

Verdant ESG helps businesses assess the evidence behind their environmental claims and communicate their achievements clearly. We bring a regulatory and evidential perspective to the language used in campaigns, packaging, websites and sustainability reports.

Our perspective

Accuracy Gives a Claim Its Credibility

A worthwhile environmental initiative can lose credibility if the claim made about it is too broad. An improvement in packaging, sourcing or production needs to be described accurately, with its scope and limitations made clear.

Our work starts with practical questions: what does the statement mean, what evidence supports it, and what will its audience understand?

We examine the connection between the claim and the underlying information, identify gaps and help businesses decide what needs to change.

Informed by legal practice

A Regulator's Eye on Every Wording

Verdant ESG draws on experience advising companies on advertising regulation and ASA matters, with ESG and greenwashing work to date principally in the fashion sector.

That experience informs our approach to environmental messaging: careful scrutiny of the wording, the supporting evidence and the overall impression a communication creates.

The same questions arise across sectors.

A claim about recycled materials, reduced emissions or responsible sourcing needs a clear basis, wherever it appears.

How we work

Practical Advice in Plain Language

We review the material you intend to publish alongside the evidence you rely upon. We explain concerns in plain language and recommend practical changes. That usually means one of three things:

  1. 01

    More specific wording

    Sometimes the wording needs to be more specific, so the claim describes exactly what has been achieved.

  2. 02

    Greater prominence

    Sometimes a qualification needs greater prominence, so the overall impression matches the evidence.

  3. 03

    Further information

    Sometimes further information is needed before the claim can be supported — and we identify what is missing.

We work with marketing, sustainability, legal and leadership teams to make those decisions easier and establish a consistent approach to reviewing claims.
